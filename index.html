Common patterns we find

Does this sound familiar?

Ferritin ↓ + TSH ↑ + D-vitamin ↓
The 'everything is normal' fatigue
Ferritin at 28, TSH at 3.1, Vitamin D at 42. Lab says normal. You're exhausted. These three together explain exactly why.
Most common pattern in our database
Ferritin ↓ + Hajhullás + Cink ↓
Hair loss with 'normal' labs
Hair loss is almost never about biotin. Low ferritin + low zinc is the real culprit 80% of the time. Most labs don't flag either.
Seen in 7 of 10 hair loss cases
Kortizol ↑ + Inzulin ↑ + Magnézium ↓
Belly fat that won't move
Eating less, exercising more – nothing works. Chronic cortisol + insulin resistance + magnesium deficiency locks fat in place. This combination is correctable.
Classic metabolic syndrome precursor
B12 ↓ + Folát ↓ + Hangulat ↓
Depression that doesn't respond to antidepressants
B12 and folate are required for serotonin synthesis. When both are borderline-low, antidepressants often don't work because the building blocks aren't there.
Found in 40% of treatment-resistant cases
TSH 2.5–4.5 + TPO ↑ + Fáradtság
Hashimoto's hiding behind 'normal' TSH
TSH 3.1 is 'normal' on most lab reports. But with elevated TPO antibodies and symptoms, it's early Hashimoto. Most GPs don't test TPO unless TSH is > 4.5.
Most underdiagnosed thyroid condition
